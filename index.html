WHAT STANDS OUT

Key project insights

Observations based on the visualisations included in your project PDF.

01

Two-wheelers lead

Two-Wheeler Scooter is the highest sales category in the displayed category chart, followed by Hatchback and Compact SUV.

02

Sales fluctuate by year

The trend chart rises to visible peaks around 2019 and 2024, with a notable dip around 2021.

03

Battery mix is varied

NMC and LFP are the two largest slices in the battery type chart, with other battery technologies also represented.

04

Markets are distributed

The regional donut shows a broadly distributed market across Africa, Asia Pacific, Europe, Latin America, Middle East, North America and South Asia.
